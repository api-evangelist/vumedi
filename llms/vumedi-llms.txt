# VuMedi

> VuMedi (Vumedi, Inc.) is a video education platform for physicians and other
> healthcare professionals, founded in 2008 and headquartered in Oakland, California.
> It hosts long-form educational videos, webinars, conference coverage and case
> discussions from key opinion leaders and academic medical centers across 25+
> clinical specialties, and monetizes through pharmaceutical and medical-device
> industry partnerships. As of 2026-08-02 VuMedi publishes no public API, no
> developer portal, no machine-readable API contract, no SDKs and no MCP server.

Generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from apis.yml and probed
public surfaces. Not published by VuMedi.

## API surface

- No public API. Probes of `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`,
  `/api-docs`, `/api/schema` and `/graphql` on `www.vumedi.com` and `vumedi.com`
  returned either 404 or the site's HTML catch-all page (confirmed against a
  control path). `api.vumedi.com`, `developer.vumedi.com` and `docs.vumedi.com`
  do not resolve.
- No GraphQL endpoint: `POST /graphql` returns the marketing HTML shell, not a
  GraphQL response.
- No MCP server, no A2A agent card, no `/.well-known/*` discovery documents.
- No first-party SDKs on npm, PyPI, or any other public registry.

## Agent posture

- [robots.txt](https://www.vumedi.com/robots.txt): VuMedi Disallows GPTBot,
  ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, CCBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended,
  Meta-ExternalAgent, Bytespider, Amazonbot, AI2Bot, Youbot and YisouSpider from
  the entire site. Automated and agentic consumption is explicitly not permitted.
- [Terms of Use](https://www.vumedi.com/public/pages/tos/)
